Add to Semble

Save links to Semble from any app on your iPhone or iPad.

Add to Semble puts an Add to Semble action in the iOS share sheet. Share a page from Safari, a post from another app, or any URL, pick a collection, add a note if you like, and it lands in your Semble library a few seconds later.

How it works

  1. Sign in once with your atmosphere account (Bluesky or any other AT Protocol PDS) using standard ATProto OAuth. The app never sees your password.
  2. The share extension writes Semble’s own record types directly into your repository, using the same network.cosmik.* lexicons Semble’s browser extension uses.
  3. Semble indexes those records from the network, so everything appears on semble.so as if you had saved it there.

There is no middle-man server: your data goes from your device to your own PDS. Read the privacy notice for the full list of who the app talks to.
