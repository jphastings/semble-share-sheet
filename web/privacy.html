Privacy notice

Add to Semble · Updated September 2026

Add to Semble is a small, open-source iOS app. It has no server, no analytics and no accounts. The developer collects no data about you.

On your device

The app stores your AT Protocol tokens, and the DPoP key that protects them, in the iOS Keychain. Only the app and its share extension can read them. They stay on this device: they are not included in backups and do not move to a new phone. The tokens go only to your own PDS and its authorisation server.

Signing out asks your authorisation server to revoke the tokens, then deletes them from the device. If the device is offline at the time, the tokens are still deleted but the server is not told.

Deleting the app does not delete them straight away. iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is removed, where no other app can read them. If you install the app again, it deletes them the first time it opens. To be sure they are revoked and gone, sign out before you delete the app.

If a link is saved while your device has no connection, the app keeps it — the link and its preview, your note, the collections you chose and any new collection's name — in its private on-device storage until it can be sent to your PDS, then deletes it. The list of your collections is kept the same way, so the app still works offline, and is replaced each time it's fetched again. Signing out does not remove these. Deleting the app does.

Who the app contacts

The app connects to these services and to no others.

  • Your PDS and its authorisation server, to sign in, to write the records you save, and to revoke your tokens when you sign out. Your account decides which servers these are.
  • plc.directory and public.api.bsky.app, to turn your handle into the address of your PDS. (An account with a did:web identity is looked up on the site that identity names instead of plc.directory.) If that fails, the app asks the website your handle is named after instead (for alice.example.com, that is alice.example.com). These look-ups are public. They use no credentials.
  • api.semble.so, for the link preview in the share sheet. The app asks Semble's public network.cosmik.card.getUrlMetadata endpoint for a title, a description and an image. It sends only the link, with no credentials, and Semble then fetches the page itself. The app makes no other request to Semble.
    A link with a query string (?…), a fragment (#…) or a username in it might carry something private, such as a sign-in code. For those links the app sends nothing until you tap Load preview.
  • The site that hosts the preview image, to show its thumbnail. The linked page chooses this site, so it can be any site. Your device fetches the image directly, over https only, so that site sees your IP address, as it would if you opened the page.

What you publish

The app writes your link, your note and your collections as records in your AT Protocol repository. Those records are public. Semble's own apps publish the same records. The app writes only to the network.cosmik.* collections that the network.cosmik.authFull permission set allows.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics, no crash reports and no telemetry.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • The app sends nothing to the developer.
