Privacy notice

Add to Semble · Updated September 2026

Add to Semble is a small, open-source iOS app. It has no server, no analytics and no accounts. The developer collects no data about you.

On your device

The app stores your AT Protocol tokens, and the DPoP key that protects them, in the iOS Keychain. Only the app and its share extension can read them. The tokens go only to your own PDS.

Sign out to delete them. Delete the app to delete them.

Who the app contacts

The app connects to these services and to no others.

  • Your PDS and its authorisation server, to sign in and to write the records you save. Your account decides which servers these are.
  • plc.directory and public.api.bsky.app, to turn your handle into the address of your PDS. These look-ups are public. They use no credentials.
  • api.semble.so, for the link preview in the share sheet. The app asks Semble's public network.cosmik.card.getUrlMetadata endpoint for a title, a description and an image. It sends only the link, with no credentials. The app makes no other request to Semble.

What you publish

The app writes your link, your note and your collections as records in your AT Protocol repository. Those records are public. Semble's own apps publish the same records. The app writes only to the network.cosmik.* collections that the network.cosmik.authFull permission set allows.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics, no crash reports and no telemetry.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • The app sends nothing to the developer.

Questions

Read the source code to check all of the above. Open an issue if something is unclear.
